First Necessities to Run

Before production begins, these are the critical requirements you must secure to legally and safely operate a purified water factory.

Priority 1
📜

Business Registration & Licenses

Register your company, obtain a business license, and secure food/beverage manufacturing permits from local authorities.

  • Business registration certificate
  • Food manufacturing license
  • Environmental clearance
  • Water extraction permit
Priority 2
🏠

Factory Location & Space

Secure a facility with adequate space (minimum 2,000-5,000 sq ft), proper drainage, and access to a reliable water source.

  • Minimum 2,000 sq ft production area
  • Separate storage & packaging zones
  • Proper ventilation & drainage
  • Proximity to water source
Priority 3
💧

Water Source Approval

Identify and test your water source. Get lab certification confirming the source meets safety standards for purification input.

  • Borehole, municipal, or spring source
  • Lab water quality analysis
  • Source sustainability report
  • Water rights documentation
Priority 4
⚡

Power & Utilities

Reliable electricity (3-phase recommended), backup generator, and plumbing infrastructure for continuous operations.

  • 3-phase industrial power connection
  • Backup generator (20-50 kVA)
  • Industrial plumbing system
  • Waste water disposal setup
Priority 5
💵

Startup Capital

Secure funding for equipment, facility setup, initial raw materials, staff salaries, and 3-6 months operating reserves.

  • Equipment & machinery budget
  • Facility lease/renovation costs
  • Working capital (3-6 months)
  • Marketing launch budget
Priority 6
👥

Trained Staff

Hire qualified personnel including a plant operator, quality control technician, and production workers with food safety training.

  • Plant manager / operator
  • Quality control technician
  • Production line workers
  • Delivery & logistics staff

Regulatory Compliance

Meet these standards and certifications to legally sell purified drinking water in your market.

🎯 Health & Safety Standards

  • WHO drinking water guidelines compliance
  • Local health department inspection passed
  • HACCP food safety management system
  • Regular water quality lab testing reports
  • Employee health certificates

📄 Required Certifications

  • ISO 22000 (Food Safety Management)
  • GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices)
  • NAFDAC / FDA / local equivalent approval
  • Environmental impact assessment
  • Fire safety certificate

🔍 Quality Control Protocols

  • Hourly TDS and pH monitoring
  • Daily microbial testing
  • Weekly full chemical analysis
  • Monthly third-party lab verification
  • Batch traceability records
